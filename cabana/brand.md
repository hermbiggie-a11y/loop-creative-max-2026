# Cabana Cold Brew — brand sheet

Fictional client for the Loop Creative demo. No real brand to clear.

## Product
Canned cold brew coffee, 12 oz slim can. Launching in Miami for summer 2027.
Flavors at launch: Original Black, Coconut Oat, Café Cubano (sweet, Little Havana special).

## Voice
Sunny, short, a little cheeky. Miami, not corporate. Headlines are 2–5 words.
Spanish is welcome (Little Havana launch). Never say "fuel", "grind" or "hustle".

## Type
Headline: Bebas Neue (PostScript name: BebasNeue-Regular). Fallback: Impact.
Body: Montserrat SemiBold (Montserrat-SemiBold). Fallback: Arial Bold.

## Logo
"CABANA" wordmark in Espresso, "COLD BREW" small caps beneath, a striped cabana-awning mark above.
Files: assets/logo.png (dark), assets/logo-light.png (light).

## Key visual
Hero can centred on a Miami beach scene (teal water, striped umbrella, palm shadow).
Headline top third, logo bottom right, safe margin 6% of the short edge.
